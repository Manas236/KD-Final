# PHOTOS — Ranked Shortlist

Which images are actually good enough to put on the website, judged by looking at them.

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-18 · **259 stills + 12 videos seen** · **41 rated usable**
**Companion to:** [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md) — that file inventories *what exists* (folders, resolutions,
EXIF). This file records *what is worth using* and *where it goes*.
**Method:** every JPG/PNG in the media folders was tiled into contact sheets and reviewed visually. CR2
RAW files, PDFs and the `Engineers Certificates/` folder were excluded.

---

## The verdict in four lines

1. **The library is 90% "finished building, daylight, no people, no machines."** Almost nothing shows
   construction happening. That rules out the L&T / Afcons "action photography" homepage — the design
   has to carry the page and let a *few* strong photographs punctuate it.
2. **The resolution is inverted.** The 6000×4000 pro DSLR shoot (97 frames) is almost entirely the
   Matunga *office block* — corridors, meeting rooms, potted plants. The photographs of actual railway
   engineering — the shed interior, the Vande Bharat trainset, the gantry cranes — are **1024–1280 px
   web exports**. The impressive subjects are the low-resolution ones.
3. **Three folders are near-duplicates of one frame.** Sanpada = 38 WhatsApp shots of one building from
   one angle. Safety / Mankhurd = 45 certificate handshakes at one wall. Matunga = 60 office interiors.
   Deduplicated, the whole library is roughly 40 distinct pictures.
4. **The best pictures are the FOBs.** Vashi and Bonkode are the only sets that are simultaneously
   high-resolution, well composed, and unmistakably *infrastructure*.

## Processing rules

`scripts/build-images.mjs` holds the picks that are actually wired up and writes the derivatives.
Run `node scripts/build-images.mjs` after changing them; it also rewrites `src/data/images.json`,
which `src/components/Picture.astro` reads for widths and intrinsic aspect ratio.

- Derivatives live in `public/img/`; **source media never enters git** ([ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md)).
- Widths: `400` card · `800` gallery · `1600` band · `2400` hero. **WebP + JPEG** today — AVIF is worth
  adding once the encode time is tuned.
- Hero and band images carry a navy `#0a1e3a` wash — every Tier-A frame is bright daylight and will not
  hold white text unaided. The homepage hero uses 35% flat plus a bottom-weighted gradient, which keeps
  the truss readable; 55–70% flat kills the photograph.
- `matunga 2.jpeg` carries a **"Shot on OnePlus" watermark** bottom-left — the script trims the bottom
  11%. Check any new pick from a phone export for the same thing.
- Every image needs alt text naming the project and the client ([SEO.md](SEO.md) §6).
- Tier B never renders wider than 800 px CSS.
