**Migrate step for the next deploy** (full steps in PRE-LAUNCH, "the 2026-11-04 launch switch")

1. `sudo mysql < db/migrate.sql`, before the build. It creates `site_settings` and `site_settings_events`.
2. As admin, once:
   `GRANT SELECT, INSERT ON kd_construction.site_settings TO 'kd_app'@'localhost';`
   `GRANT SELECT, INSERT ON kd_construction.site_settings_events TO 'kd_app'@'localhost';`
3. No `npm ci`. Leave the switch OFF. If either step is missed, the site runs OFF and `/studio/launch` says why.

**Decisions for you to overrule**

- `site_settings` has an `id` beyond your `key, value, updated_at`, and is append-only. `kd_app` has no UPDATE, and CLAUDE.md forbids widening its grants.
- Any signed-in browser sees everything with badges, in or out of edit mode, because the server cannot tell edit mode. Preview is how a signed-in person sees the public view. So if you flip ON while signed in, you will still see the placeholders; that is expected.
- With the switch ON, saving into a hidden placeholder publishes that card on the next load. This is the existing rule that a saved edit counts as answered.
- `build:release` still refuses while placeholders exist unless `HIDE_PENDING=1` is set, and that forces ON for good. To launch with the switch, PRE-LAUNCH now gives `check:site-url` + `build` + flip + `pending-report --served`.
