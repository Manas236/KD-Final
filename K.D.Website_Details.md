# K.D. Website Details

# ABOUT US

Over half a century of engineering excellence and architectural trust.
A legacy shaped by experience, a future built to endure.
"Building excellence, brick by brick."

**K.D. Constructions (Kailashchandra Dilipkumar Constructions Private Limited)** is a Navi Mumbai-headquartered infrastructure EPC company, bringing together **civil, structural steel fabrication & erection, mechanical, electrical, signalling & telecommunication, track engineering and railway infrastructure** under one integrated vision—**engineering the structures that connect communities, enable movement and shape Maharashtra's future.**

Established in **1973 in Dhule, Maharashtra**, K.D. Constructions has evolved into a trusted infrastructure partner to **Indian Railways, Central Railway, Western Railway, Mumbai Railway Vikas Corporation Limited (MRVC), CIDCO and NMMC**. From the **Vashi FOB** and station infrastructure along the **Panvel–Karjat suburban rail corridor** to **brownfield redevelopment at the Lower Parel Workshop** and the **Solapur Vande Bharat Maintenance Depot**, our projects reflect a legacy of **engineering discipline, quality execution and uncompromising commitment to safety**.

Today, K.D. Constructions operates as a **full-spectrum EPC partner**, backed by **50+ heavy equipment units, an in-house steel fabrication plant at Vindhane, Raigad, and an RMC plant at Karjat**. This integrated resource base enables **end-to-end turnkey execution**, giving us greater control over resources, supply, quality and project delivery.

Our commitment to **quality, environmental responsibility and occupational health and safety** is reinforced by **ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certifications**, along with our membership of the **Chamber of Railway Industries (Rail Chamber)**. Grounded in five decades of experience and guided by responsible engineering, K.D. Constructions continues to build **infrastructure that connects communities, strengthens mobility and moves India forward**.

# OUR HISTORY

*Three Chapters. One Legacy.*

**1973 - CHAPTER I · ORIGIN**

K.D. Constructions began in 1973 in Dhule, Maharashtra, as a partnership firm undertaking early Maharashtra state PWD works. These formative years laid the foundation for our engineering discipline, financial prudence and commitment to dependable execution - principles that continue to shape the company today.

**2004 - CHAPTER II · FORMALIZATION**

The organisation was incorporated as a Private Limited company in the month of August, 2004, marking a defining transition in our growth. The company strengthened its institutional capabilities, became pre-qualified for Indian Railway infrastructure works, and expanded its project portfolio with CIDCO, NMMC and other government bodies, establishing the foundation for larger and more complex infrastructure assignments.

**2017–PRESENT - CHAPTER III · THE MODERN ERA**

With time we have evolved into a multi-disciplinary EPC partner, expanding beyond core civil works into mechanical, electrical and track engineering. With integrated resources, owned equipment, strengthened Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) practices and a growing portfolio of complex railway and public infrastructure projects, the company is building the capabilities to deliver India's next generation of infrastructure.

Five decades behind us. A legacy beneath us. The next horizon before us.

Our philosophy rests on three enduring commitments:

- **Safety Without Exception —** **HSE is not a separate priority; it is part of every priority.** We maintain the same discipline, care and accountability on every project, at every site, every day.

# BUSINESS VERTICALS

K.D. Constructions brings together engineering, project management, manufacturing and execution capabilities under one integrated EPC framework. This enables us to deliver complex infrastructure projects with greater control over quality, resources, timelines and supply. From planning and engineering to procurement, construction, commissioning and project management, we provide integrated end-to-end solutions tailored to the requirements of railway and public infrastructure projects.

## ENGINEERING

### Civil Engineering

We deliver civil and structural infrastructure including railway stations, foot overbridges, bridges, buildings, workshops, depots, earthworks and rail corridors. Our civil capabilities form the foundation of our integrated EPC delivery.

### Steel Fabrication & Erection

We undertake **structural steel fabrication and erection** as an end-to-end discipline — from girder and truss fabrication at our own plant to **launching and erection on site, including over running railway lines**. Our heavy structural fabrication covers **foot overbridges, bridges, girders, cover-over-platform structures, steel roofing systems and large-span industrial sheds**, using both **truss and plate girder systems**. Fabrication is carried out at our **Vindhane plant, equipped with welding, bending and cutting machinery**, and **on site where a project calls for it**. With fabrication in-house and an owned crane fleet for erection, we control the full chain from shop floor to final alignment — which matters most where a launch happens within a **live railway environment with overhead OHE**. At the Vashi FOB, the structure was lifted into place with **two 650-tonne telescopic cranes**.

Demonstrated on our projects: **704.88 MT** at Matunga Workshop FOB (303 m FOB fabricated, launched and erected above a running line) · **1,412 MT** at Sanpada Carshed · **1,816 MT** at Matunga LHB Coach Maintenance Facilities · **3,046 MT** at Nhava Sheva & Uran Railway Stations · **4,686 MT** on the Panvel–Karjat Suburban Rail Corridor · FOB launching over running lines at four Harbour Line locations, with spans of **44.5 m + 24.9 m** (Vashi–Sanpada) and **38 m + 29.9 m** (Nerul–Seawood).

### Track Engineering

Our track engineering capabilities cover **permanent-way construction and associated railway track systems**, integrating track infrastructure seamlessly with civil works and broader railway operations.

### Electrical Engineering

We undertake railway and building electrical works, integrating power, electrical systems and associated infrastructure with civil and mechanical execution, including projects within operational railway environments.

### Mechanical Engineering

Our mechanical capabilities cover **railway sheds, inspection facilities, heavy repair facilities, workshops and associated mechanical installations**, supporting the development of complete railway infrastructure assets. We also **source specialised railway machinery and equipment from reputed OEMs and provide comprehensive maintenance and technical support throughout the DLP period**, ensuring equipment reliability, operational readiness and long-term performance.

### Signalling & Telecommunication

We undertake **railway signalling and telecommunication works** as part of multidisciplinary infrastructure projects, integrating critical railway communication and control systems into modern railway facilities. Our capabilities also extend to the **installation of Kavach, India's indigenous Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system**, supporting enhanced railway safety and operational reliability.

## INTEGRATED MANUFACTURING & RESOURCES

### Steel Fabrication

Our in-house steel fabrication facility at Vindhane, Raigad enables greater control over structural steel production, quality, cost and delivery schedules. Equipped with **welding, bending and cutting machinery**, the facility supports fabrication requirements for railway structures, bridges, FOBs, girders, cover-over-platforms, steel roofing systems, large-span industrial sheds and other structural works.

### Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC)

Our RMC plant strengthens our concrete supply capabilities by providing greater control over quality, consistency, availability and project scheduling, while reducing dependence on external suppliers.

### Heavy Equipment & Machinery

With a self-owned fleet of 50+ heavy equipment units, including excavators, cranes, concrete mixers, road rollers and other construction machinery, we maintain strong execution capacity and minimise dependence on rented equipment.

## ONE INTEGRATED DELIVERY MODEL

Engineering expertise + In-house manufacturing + Owned resources + Project management

Together, these capabilities allow K.D. Constructions to deliver end-to-end turnkey infrastructure solutions, from the first foundation to final commissioning-with greater control, accountability and reliability at every stage.

## Live Projects

- **Panvel–Karjat Suburban Rail Corridor | Client: MRVC**
  A **29.6 km double-line suburban railway corridor** forming part of **MUTP-III**, connecting Panvel with **Chikhale, Mohape, Chowk and Karjat**. K.D.
