### Chapter I · Origin — 1973 ★

Began in 1973 in Dhule, Maharashtra, as a **partnership firm undertaking early Maharashtra state PWD
works**. These formative years laid the foundation for engineering discipline, financial prudence and
commitment to dependable execution — principles that continue to shape the company today.

Incorporated as a **Private Limited company in August 2004**, a defining transition. The company
strengthened its institutional capabilities, became **pre-qualified for Indian Railway infrastructure
works**, and expanded its portfolio with **CIDCO, NMMC and other government bodies**, establishing the
foundation for larger and more complex assignments.

### Chapter III · The Modern Era — 2017–present ★

Evolved into a **multi-disciplinary EPC partner**, expanding beyond core civil works into mechanical,
electrical and track engineering. With integrated resources, owned equipment, strengthened **HSE**
practices and a growing portfolio of complex railway and public infrastructure projects, the company is
building the capabilities to deliver India's next generation of infrastructure.
