## How to send things

A few practical notes that save a round trip:

- **Photos: send originals, not over WhatsApp.** WhatsApp compresses images to roughly a tenth of their size. A photo that looks fine on a phone will look broken across a full-width desktop hero. Use Google Drive, WeTransfer, or email attachments.
- **Logo: vector if it exists.** Ask whoever designed the letterhead or signage — they'll have an AI, EPS, CDR, or SVG file. A PNG or a logo lifted from a PDF will blur at large sizes.
- **Documents: any format is fine.** PDF, Word, scans, photos of printed sheets — all workable.
- **Partial is better than nothing.** Approximate counts, rough dates, and unedited photos all move the build forward. Perfect information that arrives late does not.
- **Say when something doesn't exist.** A confirmed "we have no CSR programme" or "there are no safety statistics" is genuinely useful — those pages get restructured around what we do have instead of sitting half-built waiting.
