## The voice, in short

Long, composed sentences with em-dash turns. Institutional register — *integrated*, *disciplined*,
*enduring*, *responsible*. Numbers do the boasting: RCC volumes, tonnes of steel, metres of span. Human
outcome closes the argument, not opens it. The approved doc is noticeably more formal and more technical
than rev 1 — it reads as a pre-qualification document that happens to be well written, which is exactly
right for the audience.
